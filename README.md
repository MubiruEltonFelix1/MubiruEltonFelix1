## 📌 Executive Summary

I'm **Elton**, a Software Engineering student at **Mbarara University of Science and Technology (MUST)**, Uganda, working at the intersection of **deep learning, mathematics, and systems engineering**. My approach is deliberately foundational — I rebuild the architectures I use, from matrix multiplication up to attention mechanisms, because tutorials can teach syntax but only reconstruction teaches understanding.

Outside the codebase, I lead student tech communities across Mbarara and Kampala, organizing workshops that turn curious beginners into confident builders. I believe East Africa's next wave of engineers will be defined not by tool fluency, but by how deeply they understand the systems they deploy.

> *"If you can't rebuild it, you don't really understand it."*
